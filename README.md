# Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer churn in a telecommunications company using Excel, SQL, and Power BI. The objective is to understand customer retention, measure churn rate, and explore customer behavior to support better business decisions.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze customer churn and retention.
- Calculate the overall churn rate.
- Explore customer behavior across different categories.
- Identify patterns related to customer churn.
- Present key findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Dataset Overview
- **Total Records:** 7,043 customers
- **Domain:** Telecommunications
- **Data Type:** Customer demographics, services, billing, and churn information.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, analysis, and calculations.
- **MySQL:** Data querying, filtering, aggregation, and analysis.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and data visualization.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Customers | 7,043 |
| Churned Customers | 1,869 |
| Retained Customers | 5,174 |
| Churn Rate | 26.54% |

## Dashboard Analysis
The Power BI dashboard presents:
- Customer distribution by tenure.
- Monthly charges analysis.
- Churn analysis by contract type.
- Churn analysis by payment method.
- Key customer and churn KPIs.

## Key Finding
The overall customer churn rate is **26.54%**, meaning approximately one in four customers in the dataset has churned.

## Business Recommendations
- Monitor customer churn regularly.
- Understand the needs of customers who are likely to leave.
- Review contract and payment patterns.
- Develop customer retention strategies.
- Improve customer satisfaction and loyalty.
